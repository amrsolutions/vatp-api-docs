🏢 Enterprise Customers

  • Use your company subdomain
  • Example: mycompany
  • No phone parameter needed

🤝 Partners

  • Use partner subdomain
  • Include phone in all requests
  • Format: 994XXXXXXXXX

🔐 Authentication

  • Username/Password in body
  • Or use x-vatpapikey header
  • Click "Authorize" button below

🧪 Testing

  • Use "demo" for testing
  • Try example requests
  • Check response codes
